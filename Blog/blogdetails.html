A considered guide to layering for autumn

Elena Marchetti

Customer Experience, Novena

Layering well isn't about owning more — it's about owning pieces that were designed to work together. A good base layer, one considered mid layer, and an outer piece that can handle unpredictable weather will take you further than a closet full of single-season items.

Start with the base

A merino crewneck is our starting point for nine months of the year. It regulates temperature far better than cotton, resists odour between washes, and layers flat under a shirt or coat without adding bulk.

Add structure with a mid layer

This is where a wool overshirt or lightweight cardigan earns its keep. Look for a slightly oversized cut — it should sit comfortably over your base layer without pulling at the shoulders.

Finish with a coat that earns its place

Our wool-cashmere overcoat was designed specifically to sit over a mid layer without feeling restrictive. Two seasons in, it's still the piece our customers write to us about most.
