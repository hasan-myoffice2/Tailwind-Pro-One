Our Story

Fewer, better things

We started Novena with a simple frustration: most of what's sold online is designed to be replaced, not kept. So we set out to build a catalogue of considered essentials — sourced from workshops we visit in person, made from materials that age well, and priced without the usual markup games.

A decade later that principle hasn't changed. Every product on this site has been used by our own team for at least three months before it goes live.
